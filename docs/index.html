This is a research toy, not investment advice. The "Trend Score" blends a backward-looking pattern-similarity model, recent price/volume momentum, and news sentiment volume. It measures how much a stock resembles stocks that broke out before and how much it's currently moving and being talked about — it is not a probability that any stock will go up, and past patterns are no guarantee of future returns. See Methodology and the honesty panel below, which tracks whether this tool's own past scores actually predicted anything.

Methodology
